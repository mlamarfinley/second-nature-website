Business Systems

The gaps are where the money goes.

Most businesses don’t lose money on the big decisions. They lose it in the gaps — the lead that never got called back, the invoice that never went out, the handoff that lived in someone’s head. We build the connective tissue that closes those gaps.

Three kinds of help, depending on where it hurts: automations that carry the repeating work, communication systems that keep nothing waiting on someone’s memory, and AI employees that take a role outright. Every one below says what it does, what it needs from you, and whether it’s running today or built to order.

What a system takes off your desk

Chasing an unpaid invoice

A reminder ladder that runs before and after the due date, and escalates to you only when an account is genuinely stuck.

Calling back a two-week-old lead

A reply inside sixty seconds, at any hour, that qualifies and books.

Retyping receipts into your books

Parsed, coded to your chart of accounts, and flagged when it isn’t sure.

“So who’s doing what?” after every meeting

Decisions and action items extracted with owners and dates, sent to everyone who was in the room.

We don’t publish hours-saved numbers, because we’d be making them up — we have no client operations to measure yet. What we can tell you is exactly which task each system removes, what it needs from you, and how long it takes to build.

Every one of them, in detail

Lead capture and routing

Built to order

You’ve found a two-week-old inquiry nobody ever answered.

What it does

Inquiries from your form, your DMs, your email and your phone land in one pipeline. Each one is qualified, acknowledged instantly, and assigned to a person — and if it sits untouched, it escalates instead of going quiet.

What it needs from you

Wherever leads currently arrive, and a rule for who owns what.

Quotes and proposals

Built to order

Proposals take an hour to write and go out two days late.

What it does

Intake answers become a formatted proposal with your own pricing logic applied, sent for signature, tracked, and nudged until it’s signed or declined.

What it needs from you

Your pricing rules and one proposal you’re happy with, as the model.

Invoicing and payment chase

Built to order

You’re the one sending “just following up on that invoice.”

What it does

Invoices go out on completion or milestone. Reminders climb a ladder before and after the due date — polite, then firm — and only accounts that are genuinely stuck ever reach you, with an outstanding-balance summary.

What it needs from you

Your invoicing tool, your payment terms, and half an hour on the tone of the reminders.

Scheduling and dispatch

Built to order

You have field staff, or a calendar somebody manages by hand.

What it does

Real availability with buffers and travel time, automatic confirmations, reminders and reschedules, crew assignment by skill and location, and no-show slots backfilled rather than lost.

What it needs from you

Your calendar, your service areas, and how long jobs actually take.

Client onboarding

Built to order

Onboarding quality depends on who happens to be doing it.

What it does

A triggered sequence for every new client or hire — documents, contracts, credentials, access — tracked to completion, with a handoff summary when it’s done.

What it needs from you

The steps you already follow when you remember to follow them.

Document and data extraction

Built to order

Somebody on your team retypes numbers for a living.

What it does

Invoices, receipts, contracts and forms parsed straight into your CRM or accounting system. Anything it isn’t sure about is flagged for a human rather than failing silently.

What it needs from you

Samples of the documents, and where the data should land.

Reporting and KPI digests

Built to order

“How did we do last month” takes more than a minute to answer.

What it does

Pulls every tool into one brief written in plain language, on a schedule you choose, with anomalies called out rather than buried in a chart.

What it needs from you

The numbers you actually make decisions on — not every number you have.

Reviews and reputation

Built to order

You have great customers and eleven Google reviews.

What it does

A review request timed to job completion, unhappy responses routed privately to you first, and new public reviews monitored with replies drafted in your voice.

What it needs from you

Your review profiles, and how you want to handle an unhappy one.

Client follow-up that never goes cold

Built to order

Your pipeline is really a list of people you meant to call.

What it does

Every contact carries a next step and an owner. Nudges go out in your voice, and the long tail — the ones who went quiet months ago — gets worked instead of forgotten.

What it needs from you

Your contact list, wherever it lives, even if that’s a spreadsheet.

Internal handoffs with clear owners

Built to order

Work crosses roles and things get dropped in between.

What it does

Ownership is logged at every handoff, requests are routed by type rather than by whoever is least busy, and SLA timers escalate visibly instead of silently.

What it needs from you

Your team, their roles, and the handoffs that go wrong most often.

Meeting summaries and action items

Built to order

Meetings end with “so who’s doing what?”

What it does

Recording, transcript and a plain-language summary. Decisions and actions are extracted with owners and dates attached, pushed into your task tool, and recapped to everyone who was in the room.

What it needs from you

Your meeting tool and your task tool.

Team check-ins without the standup

Built to order

Nobody likes the standup and it still doesn’t surface blockers.

What it does

A short automated check-in per person, rolled into one digest. Blockers surface the same day, and overload becomes visible before somebody burns out.

What it needs from you

Your team and the cadence you want.

One inbox for every channel

Built to order

Customers reach you five ways and two of them get ignored.

What it does

Email, SMS, DMs, web chat and voicemail in a single queue sorted by urgency, with replies drafted for approval and automatic answers for the questions you answer forty times a week.

What it needs from you

Access to the channels you actually use.

A knowledge base that answers itself

Built to order

You’re the bottleneck because you’re the one who knows.

What it does

SOPs, policies and past decisions searchable in plain English, with sources cited — and every new question that comes up becomes new documentation instead of another interruption.

What it needs from you

Whatever documentation exists, however messy.

Escalation that reaches the right person

Built to order

Your “urgent” channel has been ruined by things that weren’t.

What it does

Rules for what genuinely deserves interrupting a human, sent to the right channel, to the right person, at a reasonable hour — with quiet hours honoured.

What it needs from you

What counts as urgent in your business, and who owns what.

Receptionist

Live

The phone rings while you’re under a car, on a roof, or with a customer.

What it does

Answers 24/7 in your business’s voice. Handles questions about hours, services, quotes and availability. Books into your calendar. Qualifies and routes what it shouldn’t handle itself, and sends you a transcript and summary of every call.

What it needs from you

Your hours, services, prices and policies — and a calendar to book into.

This is the one running on our own line. Call it and judge it directly.

Intake coordinator

Built to order

People express interest and then drift away before the appointment.

What it does

Turns interest into a booked, prepared appointment: collects documents, details and requirements up front, confirms, reminds, reschedules, and hands you a prepared brief before the meeting starts.

What it needs from you

What you need to know before a first meeting is worth having.

Sales development

Built to order

Leads arrive at 9pm and get answered at 11am.

What it does

Responds to a new lead in under a minute at any hour, qualifies against your real criteria, books the call, works the ones who went quiet on a genuine cadence, and logs everything to your CRM.

What it needs from you

What a good lead looks like, and what disqualifies one.

Account manager

Built to order

You find out an account is unhappy when they leave.

What it does

Scheduled check-ins on key accounts, watching for churn signals — silence, complaints, dropped usage — and surfacing renewal and upsell moments with context and drafted outreach.

What it needs from you

Your account list and what healthy usage looks like.

Operations assistant

Built to order

You are the master status picture, and you hold it in your head.

What it does

Monitors deadlines, deliverables and open loops across the business, chases the team for updates, maintains the status picture, and escalates only what’s genuinely stuck.

What it needs from you

Where work is tracked today, even if that’s several places.

Bookkeeping assistant

Built to order

The month-end close is an event rather than a routine.

What it does

Receipts and expenses captured and coded, invoices matched, discrepancies flagged, a monthly close package assembled, and your cash position reported weekly.

What it needs from you

Your accounting tool and your chart of accounts.

Prepares; does not advise. It works alongside your accountant, not instead of one.

Recruiter

Built to order

Applications pile up and good candidates go cold waiting.

What it does

Screens applications against your real requirements, schedules without the email tennis, applies consistent scorecards, and makes sure every candidate actually hears back.

What it needs from you

The role, the requirements, and your calendar.

Support agent

Built to order

You answer the same five questions all week.

What it does

Tier-one resolution drawn from your actual documentation, with sources shown. Solves the repeats outright, escalates the rest with full context, and turns every escalation into new documentation.

What it needs from you

Your documentation and your escalation path.
